      Boundaries & Methodology

      This assessment covers Scope 1 (direct), Scope 2 (purchased energy) and Scope 3 (value chain) emissions, in line with the GHG Protocol Corporate and Value Chain (Scope 3) Accounting and Reporting Standards. The organisational boundary is defined using the operational control approach: all facilities and activities over which NetZero IQ has operational control are included. Emission factors applied are consistent with recognised national inventory and GHG Protocol guidance for the reporting categories in scope.

      A.1 Calculation Methodology

      Emissions are recorded on a direct tCO₂e basis per category, calculated by the organisation from its own activity data and applicable emission factors (e.g. Defra, EPA or IPCC factors) prior to entry into this platform: Emissions (tCO₂e) = Activity Data × Emission Factor. Annual reduction projections apply the formula Remaining = Current × (1 − Annual Reduction %). Net Zero years are solved from a compound annual decline model, reaching Net Zero when emissions fall to within 2% of baseline — the practical asymptotic threshold used since percentage-based decline never reaches exactly zero.
